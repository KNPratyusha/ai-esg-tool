SUSTAINABILITY HIGHLIGHTS FY2023-24
GreenWaste Solutions Pvt Ltd, Bengaluru

ABOUT US
GreenWaste Solutions provides integrated waste management services across
Bengaluru, Mysuru, and Hubli. We employ 420 people including 95 formerly
informal waste workers integrated into our formal workforce in 2022.

ENVIRONMENTAL PERFORMANCE
Energy: We consumed 2.1 million kWh of electricity during the year.
Of this, 340,000 kWh came from rooftop solar panels installed in 2022.
Our 72 collection vehicles consumed 195,000 litres of diesel.

Emissions: We estimate our Scope 1 emissions at approximately 520 tCO2e
from vehicle fleet operations. We have not yet calculated Scope 2 or
Scope 3 emissions.

Waste processed: 48,000 metric tonnes of waste collected and processed.
32,000 MT was recovered through recycling or composting — 67% recovery rate.
14,000 MT sent to landfill. 2,000 MT hazardous waste sent to CPCB-authorised
treatment facilities.

SOCIAL PERFORMANCE
Employees: 420 total. 95 former informal waste pickers now formal employees
with EPF, ESI, and health insurance coverage since April 2022.
Safety: 8 Lost Time Injuries recorded during the year. No fatalities.
Training: Average 12 hours of safety training per employee per year.

GOVERNANCE
We have a board-approved sustainability policy since 2021.
No fines or penalties for environmental non-compliance during the year.
We do not currently publish a standalone sustainability report.
